The whole idea

Most bakeries run sugar at parity with flour. We hold it at seventy-five percent — the floor where crumb still sets but the butter, the cultured tang and the fruit stop competing with sweetness.

Below that, structure fails: the crumb goes gummy and the loaf collapses on cooling. So this isn't a health claim and it isn't a sugar-free cake. It's a measured decision, made once, and held across every recipe in the book.
